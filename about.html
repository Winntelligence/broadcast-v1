What is Winn?

A plain introduction, if this is your first visit.

What Winn is

Winn isn't a chatbot. It's a personal venture advisor — a thinking partner that has a point of view, asks the harder question, and remembers your venture. Free, for anyone with a venture on their mind: a first-time founder, an operator starting the next thing, a researcher thinking about what their work could become, or someone advising or teaching people who build. It won't write your pitch or run your business; it thinks with you — and if it thinks an idea isn't ready, it says so.

What it does

Most people arrive with a question already in hand. Winn's job is to find the one underneath it — the assumption you've been building on without testing, the question you've been working around.

You don't leave with a better answer to what you asked; you leave having found the thing you actually needed to look at. It remembers your venture between visits, so you're not starting over each time.

Getting started

Free to start, and the core stays free. Sign in so Winn can remember your venture between visits and pick up where you left off. We use your email only to save your sessions; no spam, and we don't sell your data. Rather not sign in yet? You're free to look around first.
