How Winn handles your data

The plain version, without the legal fog — here's what happens to what you type.

You don't need an account

You can use Winn anonymously. A session lives in your browser until you choose to sign in — nothing personal is required to start.

If you sign in

The only thing Winn asks for is an email, and only so it can remember your venture between visits. Your email and your sessions are stored so Winn can pick up where you left off. They are never sold, rented, or shared.

The AI behind Winn

Winn runs on Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, what you type is not used to train AI models, and Anthropic deletes it from its systems within 30 days. (Anthropic's policy.)

Your venture stays yours

Nothing you say becomes part of Winn's shared knowledge unless you explicitly choose to contribute it. Nothing is published. And when you share Winn with someone, only the invitation travels — never your work.

While Winn is early

Winn is in active development. We may review sessions to improve how it coaches. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.
